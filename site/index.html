01

A leader, not a manager.

Most multi-agent systems, like OpenClaw, put an orchestrator on top: it plans, briefs sub-agents, waits, then merges. Every plan and hand-off adds friction and slows the work, often for little gain. Super Agent has no orchestrator. The lead is a working coding agent: it starts on your task right away and hands off only what can run in parallel.

03

A usage limit doesn't stop the work.

When the working agent runs out, a free teammate continues where it stopped. The one that ran out rests until its limit resets, then rejoins.

FAQ

What is Super Agent?

A free VS Code extension that puts Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity, with as many accounts of each as you like, behind one panel and lets them work as one team in your workspace.

What does leader-based multi-agent mean?

There is no separate orchestrator or manager agent. One real coding agent leads your request and starts working immediately. It hands only independent pieces to free teammates, who work in parallel in the same folder.

Do I need several accounts?

No. Super Agent works with a single account. Add more accounts of the same agent, or other agents, whenever you want teammates and automatic hand-over at usage limits.

What happens when an agent hits its usage limit?

A free teammate continues where it stopped. Between accounts of the same CLI it continues the exact same session; across CLIs it receives the previous agent's full transcript. The agent that ran out rejoins when its limit resets.

Can I use it away from my computer?

Yes. Pair a Telegram bot and use the team from your phone: send requests, follow progress, approve actions and receive files, while Super Agent runs on your workstation.

Can it run all the time?

Yes. Super Agent keeps working on your workstation, and you reach the team from Telegram wherever you are.

How much does it cost?

Super Agent is free. You bring your own Claude, ChatGPT or Google accounts; each provider's plans, costs and usage limits apply.
